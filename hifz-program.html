Hifz Program

Memorizing the Quran is a long, rewarding journey, and it works best with a clear plan. In QuranyTime's Hifz program, each student gets a weekly memorization target, a daily revision routine, and a teacher who listens to every new portion.

New verses are kept short so they can be learned well, and older portions are reviewed on a regular cycle so they stay firm. Parents receive a simple record of what has been memorized and what still needs work.

To start memorizing, join the Quran memorization program at QuranyTime and book a free trial lesson.
