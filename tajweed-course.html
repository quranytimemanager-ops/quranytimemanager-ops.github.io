Tajweed Course

Tajweed is the set of rules for reciting the Quran correctly and beautifully. In QuranyTime's Tajweed course, a qualified tutor works with you one-to-one, listening to your recitation and correcting each rule as you meet it.

Lessons start with the letters and their points of articulation, then move to the rules of noon and meem, madd, and stopping places. Every rule is practiced with short Quran passages, so it becomes a habit rather than a list to memorize.

To begin, explore the Tajweed classes at QuranyTime and book a free trial lesson.
