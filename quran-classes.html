Quran Classes

QuranyTime's online Quran classes are live, one-to-one lessons for children and adults. Each student gets a personal plan, a qualified tutor, and a regular time slot that fits the family's week.

Lessons begin with the Arabic alphabet and move step by step to fluent reading. The teacher listens to every recitation, corrects pronunciation gently, and sets small goals for the week.
